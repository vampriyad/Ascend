FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Everything you need to know before joining ASCEND. Can't find your answer? Contact us.

Are ASCEND classes really 100% online?
Yes. All ASCEND courses take place live through Zoom. There is no physical classroom. All you need is a stable internet connection and you can join from anywhere.
Who can join ASCEND?
ASCEND is designed for Moroccan students preparing for the SAT or TOEFL, wherever they live. Students preparing for university admissions, scholarships, or exchange programs are all welcome.
Which SAT does the program prepare for?
The program prepares students for the Digital SAT, the current computer based format of the test, covering both the Mathematics and Reading & Writing sections, along with strategy and timed practice.
What does the TOEFL program cover?
All four TOEFL skills: Reading, Listening, Speaking, and Writing, plus vocabulary, test strategy, realistic practice, and individual feedback on speaking and writing responses.
How are the courses structured?
ASCEND is more than tutoring. Courses follow curricula personally prepared by the instructor: structured lessons, explanations, exercises, practice, review, and feedback, with a clear progression from your first diagnostic to test day.
When are the classes?
Schedules depend on the current session. Please contact riyadkhairoun@gmail.com or +212 644 841 837 and we will send you the current availability.
Do I need special software or equipment?
Just a computer (or tablet) with a stable internet connection, Zoom installed, and a quiet place to focus. A headset with a microphone is recommended, especially for TOEFL speaking practice.
Can I prepare for both the SAT and the TOEFL?
Yes. Many students preparing for international admissions need both tests. Get in touch and we will help you plan a preparation path that fits both goals and your timeline.
Who teaches the courses?
Every ASCEND course is prepared and taught by the same dedicated instructor. Every curriculum, every lesson, and every piece of feedback comes from one place, so nothing is outsourced and nothing gets lost.
