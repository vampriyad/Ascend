The ASCEND Method

Our approach

Every ASCEND course follows the same ascending pathway, with four stages that take students from where they are to where they want to be.

01

Assess

Understand where the student currently stands.

02

Learn

Build the knowledge and skills required for the test.

03

Practice

Apply those skills through structured exercises and realistic questions.

04

Ascend

Identify weaknesses, improve performance, and move toward the student's target.
