#🚀 ASCEND

Welcome to the official website for ASCEND.

ASCEND is an initiative created to help students discover opportunities, develop their skills, and take meaningful steps toward achieving their academic and future goals. Through guidance, resources, and accessible support, ASCEND aims to help students reach higher and unlock their potential.

##🌐 Live Website

Visit the website here:

https://vampriyad.github.io/ASCEND/

##🌍 About ASCEND

ASCEND is built around the idea that every student should have access to the knowledge, resources, and opportunities needed to move forward.

The initiative focuses on helping students navigate their academic journey, discover opportunities, strengthen their skills, and build a path toward a brighter future.
