What we cover

Four skills. One complete preparation.

The TOEFL measures how well you use English in an academic environment. ASCEND trains each skill deliberately.

Reading

Improve comprehension, vocabulary, and speed.

  • Academic passages & question types
  • Vocabulary building
  • Reading faster without losing accuracy

Listening

Practice understanding academic conversations and lectures.

  • Lectures & campus conversations
  • Note taking techniques
  • Identifying main ideas & details

Speaking

Develop clear, structured responses and improve speaking performance.

  • Response templates & structure
  • Live speaking practice on Zoom
  • Individual feedback on delivery

Writing

Practice organizing arguments, using appropriate language, and responding effectively.

  • Essay structure & organization
  • Integrated & independent tasks
  • Detailed written feedback

Test Strategy

Beyond the four skills, ASCEND teaches you how the TOEFL itself works:

  • Time management
  • Question formats
  • Response strategies
  • Realistic practice
