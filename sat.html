What we cover

Both sections. Full depth.

The Digital SAT tests two areas. ASCEND builds mastery in both, concept by concept.

Mathematics

From foundations to the hardest questions on the test, with clear explanations and worked examples.

  • Algebra
  • Advanced Math
  • Problem Solving
  • Data Analysis
  • Geometry & Trigonometry

Reading & Writing

Sharpen the reading precision and language command the Digital SAT rewards.

  • Reading comprehension
  • Vocabulary in context
  • Grammar
  • Expression of ideas
  • Evidence based reasoning
  • Rhetorical skills

Strategy

Knowing the content is half the test. The other half is knowing how to take it. ASCEND students learn:

  • Time management
  • Question solving strategies
  • Elimination techniques
  • How to approach difficult questions
  • How to review mistakes

Practice

Skills only become scores through practice. Every SAT course includes:

  • Guided exercises
  • Timed practice
  • Practice tests
  • Performance review
