About ASCEND

An independent initiative with a serious mission.

ASCEND exists to give Moroccan students access to structured, honest, high quality test preparation, wherever they live.

Our story

Why "ASCEND"?

The name represents what we believe preparation should feel like: students ascending academically, step by step, through preparation, practice, and improvement.

Standardized tests like the SAT and TOEFL open doors: universities, scholarships, and opportunities in Morocco and around the world. But quality preparation has often depended on where you live or what you can access locally.

ASCEND was created to change that. Because the courses run entirely online through Zoom, every student receives the same structured preparation, no matter where they are.

Every curriculum, every lesson, and every piece of feedback at ASCEND comes from the same dedicated instructor. We remember exactly what it feels like to sit in front of these tests, and we teach the way we wish we had been taught: clearly, honestly, and with a plan.

What we stand for

Our values

Ambition

ASCEND prepares students who aim high: for universities, scholarships, and opportunities in Morocco and beyond.

Honesty

No inflated promises, no invented numbers. Real progress, honestly reported, and the truth about what improvement takes.

Accessibility

Fully online through Zoom, so every student can prepare without limits of geography.

Structure

Courses built on curricula personally prepared by the instructor, lessons, practice, review, and progression, not improvisation.

One instructor

Everything at ASCEND, from curriculum to lessons to feedback, is handled by the same instructor. No middlemen, no rotating tutors.

Improvement

Everything ASCEND does points toward one outcome: measurable progress from where a student starts to where they want to be.
